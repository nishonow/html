About us

A clinic where doctors have time to listen

MercyMed is a multi-specialty medical center in the Ala-Too residential complex in Bishkek. Our team brings together pediatricians trained in the USA and Kyrgyzstan, a pediatric hematologist-oncologist, family doctors, a pulmonologist, an obstetrician-gynecologist, a physiotherapist, a psychologist and a doula.

Families choose us because visits are unhurried, explanations are clear, and we keep caring for patients after they leave the room. When a case needs a hospital or another specialist, we refer and follow up.

  • Individual and family care plans
  • Ultrasound, ECG and laboratory on site
  • Home visits for children
  • Consultations from 1,750 som
